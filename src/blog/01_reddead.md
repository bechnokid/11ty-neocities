title: Some Thoughts on "GTA with Angry Cowboys"
date: 2026-09-09
---
In June, I started my own game of *Red Dead Redemption 2* on the PS5 after watching my wife's own endeavors while playing the game.

This will not be a detailed review since thousands of other writers have conveyed the same feelings I have for the game much better than I ever could, but I just wanted to express my love for a game that I never thought I'd enjoy, especially not from the same company that made the *Grand Theft Auto* series.

I remember being unsure about playing it at first, assuming that its gameplay will require "high-tier gamer skills" since there's so much to keep track of. However, I decided to bite the bullet (heh), and play it anyway because I wanted to experience it myself, newbie mistakes be damned.

Suffice to say, I was *hooked*, having played consistently every day instead of updating my website or drawing as much as I wanted. {% emote 'embarrassed' %} I'm sure people would say that I took a "hiatus", but in all honesty, I just got distracted playing this game about a cowboy who loves taking care of horses more than he likes dealing with people.

Having poured at least 200 hours into *RDR2* and also starting a second playthrough, I can say that it is one of, if not **the** best video game I have ever played. As much as I love *Monster Hunter Wilds*, I cannot deny the sheer amount of love and effort that was put into making *RDR2* from its beautiful environments, addicting gameplay, well-written and complex characters, and heart-shattering story that made me sob for 500 years.

I enjoyed the game on a surface level, but there are so many layers to it that hit me really hard but in a good way! Despite its initial impression on me "just another GTA game but you play as a cowboy", *RDR2* actually left me with a slightly more positive outlook on life, which is more than I could ever ask for in a video game. {% emote 'happy' %}

I honestly wish I could talk to more people about this game, but I can understand that not a lot of people have played it, given its status as a [rated-M game](https://www.esrb.org/ratings/35888/red-dead-redemption-2/). Although games with those ratings won't stop younger people from playing and enjoying them, I feel like people can only truly appreciate *RDR2*'s story if they are at least in their mid-20's.

Despite this, I'm content with just discussing the game with my wife, sharing funny stories from our own playthroughs, and repeating inside jokes such as, "finding the ice cream badger".

With that, I end this short entry with my favorite quote from the game:

>Take a gamble that love exists, and do a loving act.
