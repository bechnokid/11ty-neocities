---
title: Some Thoughts on "GTA with Angry Cowboys"
---
In June, I started my own game of *Red Dead Redemption 2* on the PS5 after watching my wife's own endeavors while playing the game.

This will not be a detailed review since thousands of other writers can better convey the same feelings I have for the game than I ever could, but I just wanted to express my love for a game that I never thought I'd enjoy, especially not from the same company that made the *Grand Theft Auto* series.

I remember being unsure about playing it at first, assuming that its gameplay will require "high-tier gamer skills" since there's so much to keep track of. However, I decided to bite the bullet (heh), and play it anyway because I wanted to experience it myself, newbie mistakes be damned.

Suffice to say, I was *hooked*, having played consistently every day instead of updating my website or drawing as much as I wanted. I'm sure people would say that I took a "hiatus", but in all honesty, I just got distracted playing this game about a cowboy who loves taking care of horses more than he likes dealing with people.

Having poured at least 200 hours into *RDR2* and having also started a second playthrough, I can say that it is one of, if not **the** best video game I have ever played. As much as I love *Monster Hunter Wilds*, I cannot deny the sheer amount of love and effort that was put into making *RDR2* from its beautiful environments, addicting gameplay, well-written and complex characters, and heart-shattering story that made me sob for 500 years.

While I enjoyed the game on a surface level, there are so many layers to it that hit me on such a personal level. Despite its initial impression on me as a game similar to *GTA* but with cowboys, *RDR2* left me with a slightly more positive outlook on life, which is more than I could ever ask for in a video game. I honestly wish I could talk to more people about this game, but I'm content with just talking about it with my wife and gushing it here.

Even as a casual gamer, I highly recommend this game to anyone who enjoys media themed around the Wild West.

>Take a gamble that love exists, and do a loving act.
